PMS Connector Wubook

This module implement Wubook API

Features:

  • Avaliability Management
  • Restrictions Management
  • Prices Management
  • Rooms Management
  • Booking Management
  • HTTP Push Controllers
  • CRON Jobs

Important

This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning. Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production. More details on development status

Configuration

Channel extras

Some channels sell extras with the booking (parking, cot, breakfast, experiences…) and leave them out of the booking amount, so the folio does not add up to what the guest pays. The connector can add them to the folio as services. Nothing changes until it is enabled in two places:

  1. In the Backend Type, Mappings tab, OTAs list, tick Import Extras on the channels whose booking amount does not include the extras (e.g. the Mirai booking engine, Wubook channel 197). Leave it off for the rest: Booking.com, for instance, sends the city tax as an extra, and the hotel already charges its own tourist tax.
  2. In the Backend of each hotel, Extras tab, set Import Extras From. Folios created before that moment never get channel extras: reception added them by hand. Tell reception to stop typing the extras of those channels in the bookings that arrive from then on.

Each extra is matched by its name with a row of the Extras tab of the backend (also in Connectors > PMS > Channel Extras, and for the hotel managers in PMS Management > Configuration > Channel Extras, both showing the pending rows by default):

  • The names a channel has never sent before are added as pending rows, without product. Assign them the product they are imported as. Names are matched without case, accents nor punctuation, but digits count (“Parking 2” is not “Parking”). Channels change the names over time (e.g. from Spanish to English): several rows can point to the same product.
  • Set the action to Ignore, or archive the row, for the extras that must not reach the folio. Extras the channel lists at 0 as part of a package are imported at 0 unless they are ignored.
  • The Fallback Extras Product, when set, imports the extras of the pending rows anyway, named after the extra, so the folio total is right. The folios imported that way keep the fallback product when the row gets its own one later (Apply to Pending Folios does not change them): only the bookings imported from then on use it. Without a fallback product, pending extras are not imported and a folio note lists them.
  • The prices of the channel include taxes, so the products must have taxes included in the price; otherwise the extra is not imported and a folio note says so.
  • Pending extras, and the ones that could not be imported (e.g. the daily limit of the product was reached), are retried on every new version of the booking. Once fixed, Apply to Pending Folios in the row imports them on the folios waiting for them, without asking Wubook.

How the extras reach the folio:

  • All the extras of a booking go to its first room (by arrival date), even in bookings of several rooms: channels do not tell the room of an extra, and their quantity may count all the rooms.
  • The lines follow the product, as pms places them: per day products consumed before or after the night spread their quantity over the nights (from the day after the arrival when consumed after the night). The rest get a single line: on the arrival date when consumed on check-in, else on the departure date, or on the current date of the hotel when the product is not per day and the guests are already on board. The total is the one the channel charged.
  • When the channel changes or removes an extra, its service is updated or deleted, unless reception changed it by hand (product, quantity or total) or it is invoiced: then a folio note asks to review it. A service deleted by hand is never created again. If the folio already has a service of the same product added by hand, the extra is not imported, to avoid charging it twice.
  • When the channel moves the stay, the services move to the new room. Cancelled rooms do not charge their services.
  • When the channel splits a booking in several folios (one per part of the stay), every folio repeats the extras of the whole booking: they are not imported and a folio note asks reception to add them by hand to the right folio.

Payments reported by the channel

Some channels report in the booking how much the guest already paid online through the channel’s own payment gateway (today only the Mirai booking engine). The connector can register that amount on the folio as a payment. It is disabled by default and enabled per hotel and channel:

  1. Check in the backend type that the channel is mapped to the right agency.
  2. Open the Wubook backend of the hotel, tab Payments.
  3. In OTA Payment Mapping, add (or edit) the line of that agency, choose the journal and the incoming payment method where the channel settles the money, and tick Register Channel Payments.

Only one mapping per agency can have it ticked, and it needs an incoming payment method.

From the activation date, the staff must stop registering by hand the prepayments of that channel. A folio that already has payments registered by hand is never paid automatically (a note says so), but a payment registered by hand after the automatic one would be a duplicate.

Bookings imported before the activation are processed the next time they are imported (a new version sent by the channel or a manual resynchronization).

Usage

Go to connector section and create a new channel backend. Fill form and use buttons to synchronize data:

  1. Create Backend
  2. Import OTA’s Info
  3. Import Rooms
  4. Import Restrictions & Price Plans
  5. Import Availiability, Restrictions & Price Values
  6. Synch Push URL
  7. Import Reservations

** Update PMS settings to use imported plans as default plans

Avaliability, Restrictions and Pricelist are flagged when change to know when need be uploaded. All other records modifications are handled to be sent to server as it changes.

All changes are pushed every 5 minutes… and request new bookings every minute.

Modifications using calendars are pushed immediately when press “save” button.

Mirai lines in the folio internal notes

For the bookings of the Mirai booking engine (Wubook channel 197), the import can keep on top of the folio internal notes (the “internal notes” shown at the top of the folio) a few lines for reception, each one starting with [Mirai]. It is disabled by default: enable Channel Notes in the row of the Mirai channel (Wubook OTA ID 197) of the OTAs list, in the Mappings tab of the backend type. A folio gets its lines with the next import of its booking. Only Mirai passes such lines for now, so enabling it for other channels has no effect. The lines are:

  • [Mirai] Loyalty club member: Mirai flags the guest as a member of the hotel loyalty club. Mirai does not tell reliably whether the guest has just joined, so the line only states the membership, and the member id is never shown.
  • [Mirai] Upselling applied: <amount> (according to Mirai, the guest pays it at the hotel): Mirai applied an upselling. Its amount is already in the price of the booking, it is money to collect at the desk. When Wubook splits the booking in several folios, the line only goes on the first one.

The lines are written in the language of the hotel and they are rebuilt from the booking on every import of it: creation, modification, cancellation and re-import, also when Import Folios is run by arrival dates from the backend, which rewrites them for every Mirai folio of the range. Nothing is written when they did not change, and the rest of the note is kept untouched and in its order. As every line starting with [Mirai] belongs to the import:

  • staff notes must go on their own lines, never inside a [Mirai] line nor at the start of a line as [Mirai]: the next import of the booking replaces or removes such a line;
  • saving the note from a folio opened before a modification of the booking was imported can bring back the previous [Mirai] lines, which then stay until the next import of that booking.

Disabling Channel Notes stops updating the lines: those already written stay in the notes until staff removes them.

If the lines cannot be computed, the booking is imported anyway and the error goes to the server log. When the folio already existed, a note in its messages also tells reception that its [Mirai] lines may be outdated, only once: it is not posted again while the folio has it. Bookings of other channels never touch the internal notes.

How channel payments are registered

When the channel of a folio has Register Channel Payments set (see Configuration), every import of the booking compares the amount the channel reports as paid online for the whole booking with what the connector already registered on the folio, and registers the difference as a payment on the configured journal, dated on the day of the import (hotel time). Notes on the folio tell the staff what happened:

  • Payments only go up. A booking version reporting 0 paid and 0 due carries no information (the channel sends it while an online payment is pending, and when a paid booking is modified) and changes nothing. When the channel reports less than what was registered, nothing is cancelled nor refunded: a note asks to review the payments.
  • A payment registered automatically is never registered again, even if the staff cancels or edits it.
  • Online payment pending: a note shows the deadline the channel gave the guest, in hotel time. When the guest pays, the channel sends a new version of the booking and the payment is registered then. Unpaid bookings end up cancelled by the channel.
  • Cancellations keep the payments and register no refund: a note asks to review the cancellation penalty and the refund. Nothing is ever registered on a cancelled folio.
  • A booking Wubook splits in several folios reports the paid amount of the whole booking in every folio: it is not registered, a note asks to register it by hand on the right folio.
  • Not registered either, with a note: folios that already had payments registered by hand before the first automatic one, amounts in a currency different from the folio’s, and folios of a channel booking whose payment was already registered on another folio.
  • The whole amount paid is registered even if it exceeds the folio total (e.g. extras not in the folio yet); a note warns about it.
  • If the payment cannot be registered (e.g. a misconfigured journal), the booking is imported anyway and a note explains why.

Only the charges made through the channel’s own payment gateway are reported. The charges the hotel makes with its own POS terminal on the guaranteed card (first night, cancellation penalties…) never reach the channel nor Wubook and must still be registered by hand.

The amounts reported by the channel, the payment deadline and the payments registered are shown in the Wubook bindings of the folio (Connectors tab).

Room types deriving their price from another one

A room type can take its price from another room type (“Price Derived From”, in the pms module): on the days a pricelist has no rule for it, its price follows the price of its base room type. It has no items on those days, so the export of the items of the pricelist does not send those prices to Wubook. The connector pushes them to the standard plans of the regular pricelists with the export_derived_prices job (prices channel):

  • Only for the room types connected to the backend. Connections marked as “No Export” (e.g. rooms anchored to another room in Wubook) are left out.
  • Only on the days the room type derives its price from a rule of the pricelist, never from the list price of a room type, so prices maintained in Wubook are not overwritten with list prices. An item of the room type is an override: it is exported as any other item, and no derived price is pushed on its day, even if the pricing engine does not apply it (e.g. past its sale end date).
  • With the default board service added, as for the items, rounded to the cent.
  • Prices not above 0.01, which Wubook rejects, are left out and logged.

The push is enqueued when an item the derived prices depend on changes (on the dates of the item and, when it is moved to other dates, room types, properties or pricelist, on the ones it leaves; removing or moving an override pushes the derived price back), when the price derivation of a room type changes, when a room type deriving its price is connected or a plan is created in Wubook, and on “Export Pricelists” in the backend. Only the backends with some room type deriving its price connected get it. Without dates, it covers the “Flatten Forward Window” of the backend, up to the last dated item of the pricelist. Flatten pricelists already compute every price, the derived ones included.

Importing prices from Wubook does not create items for the room types deriving their price in the pricelist, which would become overrides; the items they already have are updated as usual. Removing the derivation of a room type pushes nothing for it (only for the room types still deriving their price from it): Wubook keeps its last prices until the room type gets items of its own.
