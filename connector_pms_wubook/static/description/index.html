PMS Connector Wubook

This module implement Wubook API

Features:

  • Avaliability Management
  • Restrictions Management
  • Prices Management
  • Rooms Management
  • Booking Management
  • HTTP Push Controllers
  • CRON Jobs

Configuration

Payments reported by the channel

Some channels report in the booking how much the guest already paid online through the channel’s own payment gateway (today only the Mirai booking engine). The connector can register that amount on the folio as a payment. It is disabled by default and enabled per hotel and channel:

  1. Check in the backend type that the channel is mapped to the right agency.
  2. Open the Wubook backend of the hotel, tab Payments.
  3. In OTA Payment Mapping, add (or edit) the line of that agency, choose the journal and the incoming payment method where the channel settles the money, and tick Register Channel Payments.

Only one mapping per agency can have it ticked, and it needs an incoming payment method.

From the activation date, the staff must stop registering by hand the prepayments of that channel. A folio that already has payments registered by hand is never paid automatically (a note says so), but a payment registered by hand after the automatic one would be a duplicate.

Bookings imported before the activation are processed the next time they are imported (a new version sent by the channel or a manual resynchronization).

Usage

Go to connector section and create a new channel backend. Fill form and use buttons to synchronize data:

  1. Create Backend
  2. Import OTA’s Info
  3. Import Rooms
  4. Import Restrictions & Price Plans
  5. Import Availiability, Restrictions & Price Values
  6. Synch Push URL
  7. Import Reservations

** Update PMS settings to use imported plans as default plans

Avaliability, Restrictions and Pricelist are flagged when change to know when need be uploaded. All other records modifications are handled to be sent to server as it changes.

All changes are pushed every 5 minutes… and request new bookings every minute.

Modifications using calendars are pushed immediately when press “save” button.

Payments reported by the channel

When the channel of a folio has Register Channel Payments set (see Configuration), every import of the booking compares the amount the channel reports as paid online for the whole booking with what the connector already registered on the folio, and registers the difference as a payment on the configured journal, dated on the day of the import (hotel time). Notes on the folio tell the staff what happened:

  • Payments only go up. A booking version reporting 0 paid and 0 due carries no information (the channel sends it while an online payment is pending, and when a paid booking is modified) and changes nothing. When the channel reports less than what was registered, nothing is cancelled nor refunded: a note asks to review the payments.
  • A payment registered automatically is never registered again, even if the staff cancels or edits it.
  • Online payment pending: a note shows the deadline the channel gave the guest, in hotel time. When the guest pays, the channel sends a new version of the booking and the payment is registered then. Unpaid bookings end up cancelled by the channel.
  • Cancellations keep the payments and register no refund: a note asks to review the cancellation penalty and the refund. Nothing is ever registered on a cancelled folio.
  • A booking Wubook splits in several folios reports the paid amount of the whole booking in every folio: it is not registered, a note asks to register it by hand on the right folio.
  • Not registered either, with a note: folios that already had payments registered by hand before the first automatic one, amounts in a currency different from the folio’s, and folios of a channel booking whose payment was already registered on another folio.
  • The whole amount paid is registered even if it exceeds the folio total (e.g. extras not in the folio yet); a note warns about it.
  • If the payment cannot be registered (e.g. a misconfigured journal), the booking is imported anyway and a note explains why.

Only the charges made through the channel’s own payment gateway are reported. The charges the hotel makes with its own POS terminal on the guaranteed card (first night, cancellation penalties…) never reach the channel nor Wubook and must still be registered by hand.

The amounts reported by the channel, the payment deadline and the payments registered are shown in the Wubook bindings of the folio (Connectors tab).
