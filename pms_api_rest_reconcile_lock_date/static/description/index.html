PMS API REST - Reconcile Lock Date

Editing a posted invoice from the PMS app (PATCH /invoices/p/<id> of pms_api_rest) replaces it with a new one. pms_api_rest does it the core way: a credit note issued with cancel=True, which starts by unreconciling the original, so the payment that settled it is released. When that payment belongs to a closed period, the customer balances of a month that is already closed change.

This module plugs account_reconcile_lock_date’s _reissue_invoice() into that edit. Where unreconciling does harm – the invoice or what it is reconciled with belongs to a locked period, it is a down payment invoice, or something other than a payment settles it – the original keeps its payment, the credit note is issued with cancel=False, and the money the original collected moves onto the new invoice with a balance transfer entry dated today. Otherwise it is still the core reversal, and the new invoice takes over the very payments the original had, not whatever the folio autoreconciliation would match. Whatever the new invoice still owes then takes the folio’s free payments, as usual.

Odoo installs it by itself when the second of pms_api_rest and account_reconcile_lock_date is installed. On a database that already has both, install it explicitly. A company without a transfer journal keeps the core behaviour.

Payments follow the same rule (PATCH /transactions/p/<id> and the channel updates of a folio). A payment of a closed period – its own date, or that of something it settles – is never reset to draft: its reference is written in place; a change of amount is registered as a payment or a refund for the difference, dated today and linked to it (the channels see the amount with its corrections); its customer cannot change when it settles an invoice, whose own edit moves the money, and moves with a transfer entry otherwise; its journal cannot change; its date cannot change, except for a payment of an open month locked only by what it settles, moved in place to another open date. No payment or refund can be moved or dated into a closed period, which the core would otherwise do silently. In an open period, an edited payment is reconciled again with the invoices it settled (pms_api_rest). The members of the bypass group keep the usual behaviour.
